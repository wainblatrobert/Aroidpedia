Status key: **held** = file in the research pack (see `data/papers.csv` /
`data/protologue-scans.csv`); **login** = IAS members' PDF on aroid.org;
**paywalled**; **blocked** = site refuses scripted download (fetch by hand);
**not found** = no online copy located; **cited only** = known from citation.
