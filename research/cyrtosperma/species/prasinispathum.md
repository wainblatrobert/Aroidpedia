# Cyrtosperma prasinispathum A.S.D.Irsyam & M.R.Hariri

**Status:** accepted (POWO/WCVP).
**Protologue:** Hariri, M.R., Irsyam, A.S.D., Ariati, S.R., Raynalta, E., Setyanti, D. & Supriyatna, I. (2024). *Cyrtosperma prasinispathum*: a new fascinating Aroid species from Papua, Indonesia. Webbia 79(2): 291–294 (3 September 2024). doi:10.36253/jopt-16076. Open access, CC BY. **Held in full.**
**Type:** MRH 777, prepared 14 March 2024 from a living plant at Bogor Botanic Gardens–BRIN; origin Indonesia, Southwest Papua Province, Sorong Regency (holotype FIPIA, isotype BO). The plants derive from seed (DW1091) collected by D. Widyatmoko at Taman Wisata Alam Sorong, 27 m a.s.l., 11 June–9 July 2007, germinated and grown at Bogor, and long labelled *C. hambalii*.
**Informal group:** Macrotum group.
**Etymology:** Latin *prasinus*, leek-green, and *spathum*: the leek-green spathe.
**Conservation:** authors propose Data Deficient (known only from the Bogor living collection).

## Diagnosis (Hariri et al. 2024)

Geographically associated with *C. macrotum* but differs in: unarmed abaxial
leaf surface (vs armed); oblanceolate posterior lobes (vs ovate to broadly
lanceolate); elliptic anterior lobe (vs ovate to deltoid); deflected spathe
(vs deflected or shortly decurrent); entire spathe margin (vs undulate);
leek-green spathe (vs white-yellowish green/brown to purple); yellowish-green
to yellow spadix (vs pale yellow to green); consistently tetramerous flowers
(vs hexa- to tetramerous or mixed).

## Description (Hariri et al. 2024: 292)

Small herb 21–30 cm. Leaves c. 5–9 per crown, clustered; sheath 2–4 cm,
brown; petiole slender, 5–25 cm, brown with black patches, armed with
straight, downward and mostly upward spines 1–4 mm, yellowish brown; blade
sagittate-hastate, anterior lobe elliptic, shorter than the posterior lobes,
3.5–12 × 2–6.2 cm, pointing downward (flat in juvenile leaves); posterior
lobes oblanceolate, 4.2–17 × 2–5.5 cm, pointing downward, sinus 60–80°;
adaxial surface dark green, abaxial glaucous. Peduncle 50–73 cm, brown with
black patches, armed with minute spines 1–2 mm. Spathe broadly open,
lanceolate, deflected, 10.9–11.5 × 2.5–2.9 cm, leek-green. Spadix 35–45 × 6–7
mm, yellowish green turning yellow; stipe 3–6 mm, leek-green. Flowers
bisexual, perigoniate; tepals 4, free, green turning yellow; stamens 4, free,
filaments flat and broad, anthers c. 0.5 mm, white, not exserted at anthesis;
pollen white; ovary 1-locular, brown; stigma discoid, sessile, brown.

## Distribution and habitat

- **POWO:** W. New Guinea (native).
- Known from the 2007 Sorong collection only (lowland, 27 m); no specimens or observations in GBIF or iNaturalist under this name (the 38 genus-level iNat observations from Indonesia could include it).

## Notes

- The paper's Figure 1 (A–H habit, petiole spines with up-, down- and straight-pointing tips, spathe from three angles, spadix, immature flowers, leaf; I *C. macrotum* leaf; J *C. hambalii* leaf) is CC BY and rendered in the protologue pack as page 293 — usable with attribution to Hariri et al. 2024.
- With this species Hariri et al. count the Macrotum group at four: *C. beccarianum*, *C. macrotum*, *C. hayii*, *C. prasinispathum*, and the genus at 15.
- The authors note its "striking-colored spathe" makes it "an important candidate for horticultural plants".
