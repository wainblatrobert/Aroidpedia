# Cyrtosperma timikense Imran & A.Hay

**Status:** accepted (POWO/WCVP).
**Protologue:** Hay, A. & Imran (2020). A third enormous New Guinea lasioid — *Cyrtosperma timikense*, a new species from southern Papua Province, Indonesia. Aroideana 43(3–4): 97–107.
**Type:** not yet seen (Aroideana PDF is IAS members-only).
**Informal group:** by the authors' comparison, Cuspidispathum group.
**Etymology:** from Timika, Mimika Regency, southern Papua, the type region.
**IDs:** IPNI 77214865-1 · POWO urn:lsid:ipni.org:names:77214865-1 · WCVP 546918 · WFO wfo-1000022045.

## What the sources in hand say

- **Abstract (as indexed):** described from the Mimika Regency of Indonesia's Papua Province and "distinguished from *Cyrtosperma cuspidispathum* Alderw., which species it most resembles". The title places it with *C. cuspidispathum* and *C. giganteum* as the third "enormous" New Guinea lasioid, so expect a plant of several metres.
- **POWO:** native to W. New Guinea.
- **Hariri et al. (2024)** cite it in their count of 14 species (before *C. prasinispathum*).
- **GBIF:** no records. **iNaturalist:** none identified. Seed is offered commercially (e.g. onszaden.com lists "Cyrtosperma timikense"), so cultivated plants exist.
- The same Aroideana issue carries Hay & Imran's *C. giganteum* rediscovery (p. 4) and Hay's *C. johnstonii* typification note (p. 108): one PDF fetch covers three species.
