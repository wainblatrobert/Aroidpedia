# Cyrtosperma bougainvillense A.Hay

**Status:** accepted (POWO/WCVP).
**Protologue:** Hay, A. (1988). Blumea 33: 441–442, Fig. 6 (drawn from Hay 2051).
**Type:** R. Schodde & L. Craven 3837, Papua New Guinea, North Solomons Province, Bougainville Island, Maide Gorge, lower south slopes of Lake Loloru crater, c. 15 miles N of Buin, 14 August 1964 (CANB, holotype; A, K, L, LAE isotypes). Imaged: K000499251, K000499252, A 00025749, L 0041890.
**Diagnosis (Latin):** differs from *C. merkusii* in the more or less flat spathe, the twisted stony seed, the vermilion stigma and the glaucous spadix.
**Informal group:** Merkusii group.
**IDs:** IPNI 935716-1 · POWO urn:lsid:ipni.org:names:935716-1 · WCVP 54871 · WFO wfo-0000936201 · iNat taxon 937781.

## Description (Hay 1988: 441)

Robust, usually solitary herb 1–2.4 m; rhizome often long and creeping.
Leaves 2–5, sagittate; petioles prickly, green mottled white; spines slender,
without conspicuously broad bases, slightly curved, scattered or in short
comb-like clusters; geniculus up to 14 cm; sheath papery-fibrous, about a
fifth of the petiole; posterior lobes 40–75 × 20–30 cm, tips not acuminate;
anterior lobe 35–50 cm long, to c. 60 cm wide, with spreading costae.
Peduncle to 2 m, usually shorter than the petioles, prickly. Spathe blackish
purple to almost white, broadly lanceolate, to 27 × 6.5 cm, widely open to
the base, somewhat deflected from the spadix, not shielding it from the side,
the upper part sometimes falling over the spadix. Spadix stipitate for c. 6
mm with the insertion running c. 1 cm up the spathe; fertile part to 11.5 × 1.6
cm in flower, purplish to glaucous-blue. Flowers hexamerous; anthers dirty
yellow, exserted at male anthesis; receptive stigmas bright scarlet; ovules
(1 or) 2. Fruiting spadix becoming horizontal to pendulous, dark bluish to
black. Fruit obpyramidal, c. 8 × 8 mm, orange, truncate. Seed tightly curved
over on itself; seed coat very thick, stony, somewhat rough.

The seed is distinctive: campylotropous as in the rest of the genus, but the
micropylar and chalazal ends overlap so that it is helical and the funicle
appears to enter through a hole in the side (Hay 1988: 442).

## Distribution and habitat

- **POWO:** Solomon Is. (native) — TDWG "Solomon Is." includes Bougainville.
- **Hay 1988:** restricted to the actively volcanic, cupriferous island of Bougainville; montane rainforest and regrowth in moist gullies at 600–2,000 m (Panguna, Kieta, Kapikavi, Arawa). Locally abundant yet unrecorded elsewhere in the Solomon chain — "possibly the species is restricted to copper-bearing soils". Waterhouse 571, "Iru (Mt. area), Siwai, Sept 1931", may belong here.
- **GBIF:** 19 records (16 specimens, 5 georeferenced), 1931–1967; Papua New Guinea 14, Solomon Islands 5; elevation 610–884 m.
- **iNaturalist:** none.

## Notes

- Its closest relatives on Hay's reading are *C. merkusii* and the Louisiade endemics; Bougainville's political and mining history means there have been no collections since 1967 in GBIF.
- No modern photographs found.
