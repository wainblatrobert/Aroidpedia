# Cyrtosperma kokodense A.Hay

**Status:** accepted (POWO/WCVP).
**Protologue:** Hay, A. (1988). Blumea 33: 447–448, Fig. 9 (drawn from NGF 23585 and Cheesman 3).
**Type:** C.E. Carr 16194, Papua New Guinea, Northern (Oro) Province, Kokoda, 22 March 1936 (L, holotype; B, BM, CANB, SING isotypes). Imaged: L 0041895, L 0041896, G 00191357 (Geneva).
**Diagnosis (Latin):** differs from *C. cuspidispathum* in mostly straight spines, an armed blade, a spathe not convolute at the base and not long-acuminate at the apex.
**Informal group:** Cuspidispathum group.
**Etymology:** from Kokoda, the type locality.
**IDs:** IPNI 935717-1 · POWO urn:lsid:ipni.org:names:935717-1 · WCVP 54888 · iNat taxon 937775.

## Description (Hay 1988: 447)

Herb to 1.5 m. Blade markedly hastate to sagittate, c. 40–100 cm in flowering
plants; anterior lobe to 30 cm from tip to sinus; posterior lobes obliquely
narrowly ovate, to c. 75 cm, with rather fine and numerous costae diverging
from the midribs at c. 35°. Petioles sometimes mottled; armature sparse,
short, slender, to 4 mm, straight to upcurved. Spathe to 25 × 2.5 cm,
lanceolate, the lower 5 cm clasping the spadix without overlapping, the middle
deflected, the upper part spirally twisted, tip not or hardly acuminate,
purple, paler within with yellowish streaks; spadix stipitate for 1 cm,
4.5–5.5 cm × c. 6 mm in flower, cylindrical. Flowers hexamerous; anthers not
exceeding the tepals at male anthesis; stigmas brown (dried), papillate; ovary
1(–2)-ovulate. Ripe fruit and seed unknown; Hay expects the fruit to be
expelled from the spadix as in *C. cuspidispathum*.

## Distribution and habitat

- **POWO:** New Guinea (native).
- **Hay 1988:** Papua New Guinea, Northern and Central Provinces (Kokoda, Kokoda Trail, Boridi), rainforest undergrowth and stream sides at around 400 m. Map: Fig. 8.
- **GBIF:** 11 records (10 specimens, 5 georeferenced), 1933–1973; elevation 350–1,190 m, median 368 m. Carr, Cheesman, Foreman & Vinas.
- **iNaturalist:** none.
