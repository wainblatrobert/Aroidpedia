# Cyrtosperma hambalii A.Dearden & A.Hay

**Status:** accepted (POWO/WCVP).
**Protologue:** Dearden, A. & Hay, A. (2001). A new species of *Cyrtosperma* (Araceae) from West Papua. Aroideana 24: 102–104.
**Type:** not yet seen — in the protologue (Aroideana PDF is IAS members-only).
**Informal group:** not assigned by Hay (1988, predates it); Hariri et al. (2024) do not place it in the Macrotum group.
**Etymology:** not confirmed from the protologue; presumably honours Gregori G. Hambali, the Bogor aroid specialist — check when the PDF is in hand.
**IDs:** IPNI 20001020-1 · POWO urn:lsid:ipni.org:names:20001020-1 · WCVP 54883 · WFO wfo-0000936214 · iNat taxon 937776.

## What the sources in hand say

- **POWO:** native to SW New Guinea.
- **Hariri et al. (2024, Webbia)** — the *C. prasinispathum* paper: living plants at Bogor Botanic Gardens collected in Sorong (Southwest Papua) in 2007 had been labelled *C. hambalii*; on examination they proved to be a different, new species. Their Fig. 1J shows a *C. hambalii* leaf for comparison ("the three leaves show distinguishable characteristics among different species"). So *C. hambalii* is (a) in cultivation at Bogor and (b) sufficiently similar to *C. prasinispathum* to have been confused with it; *C. prasinispathum* is a small (21–30 cm), tetramerous, leek-green-spathed plant.
- A 2023 figure on ResearchGate (fig. 1 of publication 375336461) is captioned "*Cyrtosperma hambalii*. Flowering plant showing characteristic 'rabbit ear' leaves" — a lead to a recent illustrated treatment, not verified.
- **iNaturalist:** 6 observations (2023–2026), all flagged wild, 4 research grade, Indonesian Papua; 16 photos, 14 copied (CC BY-NC-SA 10, CC BY-NC 4); 2 all-rights-reserved listed only. These are currently the best pictures of the species available to the build.
- **GBIF:** 1 record (a 2025 human observation, Indonesia); no specimens indexed.
