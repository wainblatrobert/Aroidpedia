# Cyrtosperma gressittiorum A.Hay

**Status:** accepted (POWO/WCVP). Spelling: published as *gressittorum*; IPNI keeps the original spelling, POWO/WCVP correct it to *gressittiorum* (genitive plural of Gressitt, "of the Gressitts"). The site should follow POWO but record the original spelling.
**Protologue:** Hay, A. (1988). Blumea 33: 439, Fig. 4 (drawn from the type).
**Type:** L.J. Brass 28049, Papua New Guinea, Milne Bay Province, Sudest (Tagula) Island, Rambuso, 11 September 1956 (L, holotype; A isotype). Imaged: L 0041894, A 00025751.
**Diagnosis (Latin):** differs from all other species of *Cyrtosperma* in the hooded (cucullate) spathe.
**Informal group:** Merkusii group.
**Etymology:** commemorates the entomologist and biogeographer J. Linsley Gressitt and his wife Margaret, who died together in an air crash in China in April 1982; Gressitt introduced Hay to tropical vegetation at the Wau Ecology Institute.
**IDs:** IPNI 935714-1 · POWO urn:lsid:ipni.org:names:935714-1 · WCVP 54882 · iNat taxon 937777.

## Description (Hay 1988: 439)

Herb to 70 cm. Petiole to 60 cm, sheath papery c. 15 cm, spines sparse,
straight, needle-like, to 3 mm; petiole variegated green and white. Blade
hastate-sagittate, sparsely armed below, c. 30 cm; anterior lobe subequalling
the posterior; posterior lobes slender, to 6 cm wide, their midribs naked in
the sinus for c. 3 cm, lesser venation somewhat prominent below; anterior
costae arising near the sinus, curvinerved, running to the margin near the
tip. Peduncle exceeding the petiole, to 90 cm. Spathe lanceolate, c. 7 cm,
hood-forming in the upper quarter, green, major venation parallel and
distant, secondary venation reticulate; spadix orange, 4 cm, with a 5 mm stipe
adnate to the spathe. Flowers tetramerous; anthers exserted at male anthesis,
apparently retracting afterwards; stigmas hardly exceeding the tepals; ovule
solitary. Fruit and seed unknown.

## Distribution and habitat

- **POWO:** New Guinea (native) — Sudest (Tagula) Island, Louisiade Archipelago.
- **Hay 1988:** known only from the type, rainforest on the crest of a ridge at 150 m. Map: Fig. 3.
- **GBIF:** 3 records, all duplicates of Brass 28049 (1956).
- **iNaturalist:** none.

## Notes

- Still known from a single gathering (as far as the sources pulled show). The hooded spathe is unique in the genus.
