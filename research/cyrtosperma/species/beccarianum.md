# Cyrtosperma beccarianum A.Hay

**Status:** accepted (POWO/WCVP).
**Protologue:** Hay, A. (1988). Blumea 33: 455–456, Fig. 13 (drawn from van Royen 4792).
**Type:** Beccari P.P. s.n., Irian Jaya [West Papua], Batanta, July 1875 (FI, holotype) — the Batanta specimen Engler had cited under *C. macrotum* in 1879.
**Diagnosis (Latin):** differs from *C. macrotum* in the erect posterior lobes of the leaf and the more or less flat, ovate spathe.
**Informal group:** Macrotum group.
**Etymology:** for Odoardo Beccari, collector of the type.
**IDs:** IPNI 935719-1 · POWO urn:lsid:ipni.org:names:935719-1 · WCVP 54870 · WFO wfo-0000936200 · iNat taxon 937782.

## Description (Hay 1988: 455–456)

Feeble to moderately robust herb 40–150 cm. Leaves 3–7; sheath papery,
fibrous, about a quarter of the petiole; petiole sparsely armed to unarmed,
rarely rather densely spiny; blade variable, held with the anterior lobe down
and the posterior lobes up, usually sagittate; anterior lobe triangular, c. 10
cm long, 8 cm wide at base; posterior lobes lanceolate, c. 25 × 7.5 cm,
midribs naked in the sinus for up to 4 cm, sinus 30–100°. Peduncle
subequalling to exceeding the petioles, more sparsely armed, like them
blotched and marbled green, brown, pink and white, or plain brown or green,
often drying greyish. Spathe broadly to narrowly ovate, opening broadly
boat-shaped to flat, white, pale pink or pale yellow, acuminate, 1.8 × 5 to
4.8 × 9 cm; spadix sessile, adnate to the spathe for c. 5 mm, 4 × 20 mm in
flower, 1.5 × 6 cm in fruit, pale yellow. Flowers tetramerous; ovule solitary.
Fruit orange, globose, protruding from the spadix as it swells. Seeds tightly
curved, smooth.

## Distribution and habitat

- **POWO:** New Guinea (native).
- **Hay 1988:** southwest and west New Guinea (Batanta, Ramoi, Lorentz River, Digul River, Merauke area, Fly and Palmer Rivers, Kiunga, Lake Murray), at low altitude in streams and ditches, swamp forest and gallery-forest undergrowth, in shade. "Ecology and leaf form of most individuals of this species combine to make this an example of a rheophyte." Map: Fig. 14.
- **GBIF:** 31 records (27 specimens, 12 georeferenced), 1907–1999; Papua New Guinea 17, Indonesia 13; elevation 24–210 m, median 73 m.
- **iNaturalist:** none identified to species (some of the 38 genus-level observations may belong here).

## Notes

- Hariri et al. (2024) list it in the Macrotum group with *C. macrotum*, *C. hayii* and *C. prasinispathum*; like *C. prasinispathum* it is small, tetramerous and pale-spathed, so the two will need careful separation (Hariri et al. compare *prasinispathum* only with *macrotum*).
