## What the sources in hand say

- The title gives the diagnostic character: **downward-pointing spines**, "unique" in the genus. In Hay's 1988 generic key spines "mixed straight and down-turned" characterise *Podolasia*, while *Cyrtosperma* has spines straight and up-turned or all up-turned; *C. hayii* breaks that distinction. (Hariri et al. 2024 describe *C. prasinispathum*'s spines as "straight, downward, and mostly upward", so a few down-turned spines occur there too.)
- **Hariri et al. (2024):** a member of the Macrotum group with *C. macrotum*, *C. beccarianum* and *C. prasinispathum*; the group is defined by a spathe not convolute below and tetra-, penta- or hexamerous flowers.
- **POWO:** native to W. New Guinea; the type region is Southwest Papua Province (Sorong area), as for *C. prasinispathum*.
- **iNaturalist:** 1 observation (2026, wild) with 4 CC BY-NC photos, all copied.
- **GBIF:** no records.
