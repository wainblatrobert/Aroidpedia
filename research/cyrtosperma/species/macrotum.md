# Cyrtosperma macrotum Becc. ex Engl.

**Status:** accepted (POWO/WCVP).
**Protologue:** Engler in Beccari, Bull. Reale Soc. Tosc. Ortic. 4: 295 (1879), 'macrota'; redescribed and illustrated in Beccari, Malesia 1: 279 (1882), Tav. XXIV figs 1–6 (Hay cites "23, t. 23"; the plate itself is numbered XXIV and the text is no. 39 on p. 279).
**Type:** Beccari P.P. 619, Irian Jaya [West Papua], Andai (FI, lectotype, selected by Hay 1988). Hay showed that the Batanta specimen also cited in the protologue is a different species, which became the holotype of *C. beccarianum*, and that D'Albertis' Fly River collection is a mixture (leaf of this species, inflorescence of *Lasia spinosa*).
**Informal group:** Macrotum group.
**IDs:** IPNI 86824-1 · POWO urn:lsid:ipni.org:names:86824-1 · WCVP 54890 · WFO wfo-0000936222 · GBIF 206541535 · iNat taxon 937774.

## Description (after Hay 1988: 451)

Moderately robust herb to 1.3 m; rhizome plagiotropic or very short, to 6 cm
diameter. Leaves 3–several, hastate to sagittate; petioles divergent from the
base, prickly, mottled or not; blades to c. 90 × 50 cm, held horizontally or
with the posterior lobes down, more or less coriaceous, armed beneath
(sometimes heavily) and sometimes on the margins, usually all facing the same
way, the primary and secondary veins (sometimes very) prominent beneath;
posterior lobes markedly exceeding the anterior, asymmetrically ovate to
broadly lanceolate, naked in the sinus for up to c. 5 cm. Inflorescence
solitary, occasionally two in succession, on peduncles slightly exceeding the
petioles. Spathe narrowly lanceolate, to c. 15 × 3 cm, occasionally spiny at
the base and along the midline outside, white-yellowish green/brown to
purple, its insertion shallowly cup-like or with the margins deflected or
shortly decurrent on the peduncle; spadix c. 6–12 cm × 5 mm, pale yellow to
green, free-stipitate, adnate-stipitate or sessile. Flowers hexamerous or
tetramerous or mixed (some pentamerous); ovule solitary; anthers not exserted;
fruit sessile, ovoid, orange; seed smooth, asymmetrical.

Alderwerelt's *C. syapense* (1920: 374), from a young Bogor plant flowering
and fruiting January 1919, adds: petiole 25–50 cm, copiously but not densely
prickly, dark green below with irregular transverse streaks; spathe 10–12 ×
3–3.5 cm, pale dirty yellow inside with pale green veins; spadix 5–7.5 cm,
yellowish; tepals and stamens 4; berries obovoid, red below, green above;
testa smooth.

## Distribution and habitat

- **POWO:** New Guinea (native).
- **Hay 1988:** New Guinea, Aru Islands, d'Entrecasteaux Islands (Normanby, Fergusson); lowland swamp forest, lowland rainforest and lower montane forest. Three disjunct areas: Morobe/Madang; Milne Bay; southern and western Irian Jaya with Western Province PNG. Hay (1988: 453) describes how leaf texture, spadix stipe, flower merosity and spathe insertion vary between and within these areas, declines to recognise the Milne Bay and Morobe/Madang entities formally, and suggests Pleistocene fragmentation of a once continuous lowland species.
- **GBIF:** 167 records (150 specimens, 63 georeferenced), 1907–2026; Papua New Guinea 95, Indonesia 49; elevation 7–1,100 m, median 200 m. Collectors include Brass, Takeuchi, Barrau, Nicolson.
- **iNaturalist:** 10 observations (2023–2026), all flagged wild, 5 research grade, mostly Indonesian Papua; 17 photos, 14 copied (CC BY-NC 10, CC BY 4).

## Notes

- Hariri et al. (2024) use *C. macrotum* as the comparison for *C. prasinispathum*: *macrotum* has an armed abaxial leaf, ovate to broadly lanceolate posterior lobes, an ovate to deltoid anterior lobe, a deflected or shortly decurrent spathe with undulate margin, white-yellowish-green/brown to purple, a pale yellow to green spadix and hexamerous to tetramerous or mixed flowers. Their Fig. 1I shows a *C. macrotum* leaf.
- The spadix reorients to horizontal in fruit (Hay 1988: 446).
