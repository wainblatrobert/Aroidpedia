# Cyrtosperma carrii A.Hay

**Status:** accepted (POWO/WCVP).
**Protologue:** Hay, A. (1988). Blumea 33: 450–451, Fig. 10 (drawn from Carr 12307).
**Type:** C.E. Carr 12307, Papua New Guinea, Central Province, Koitaki, 1500 ft, 15 March 1935 (BM, holotype; L, LAE, SING isotypes). Imaged: BM000958554, BM000958555, L 0041892.
**Diagnosis (Latin):** differs from all other species in the very broadly ovate spathe convolute at the base and the conoid style.
**Informal group:** Carrii group (alone).
**Etymology:** for Cedric Errol Carr, who collected the type and died of blackwater fever in Port Moresby in 1936.
**IDs:** IPNI 935718-1 · POWO urn:lsid:ipni.org:names:935718-1 · WCVP 54873 · iNat taxon 430921.

## Description (Hay 1988: 450)

Herb to 55 cm. Leaves several, hastate, armed on the petiole and on the
underside and margins of the blade; petioles to 50 cm with an 11 cm sheath;
blade to 40 cm, coriaceous; spines sparse, straight to very slightly curved,
to 5 mm; anterior lobe oblong, gradually acuminate, subequalling the
posterior lobes; two anterior costae emerging near the sinus, curvinerved,
running to the margin near the tip; posterior lobes broadly lanceolate, their
midribs naked in the sinus for c. 3 cm. Spathe broadly ovate, clasping in the
lower fifth, c. 12 cm long and to 8 cm wide flattened, deep reddish brown with
yellow, widely reticulate venation that persists as a "skeleton" around the
fruiting spadix; spadix with a 4 mm stipe adnate to the spathe, fertile part
c. 2.5 cm (printed "25 cm", evidently a slip) × 5 mm. Flowers tetramerous;
anthers not exserted; stigmas raised 1–2 mm on the conical apex of the ovary.
Fruit ovoid, capped by the style and stigma. Seed smooth.

## Distribution and habitat

- **POWO:** New Guinea (native).
- **Hay 1988:** Papua New Guinea, Central Province (Koitaki, Mafulu, Musgrave, Manumu, Kemp Welch headwaters); lowland and lower montane rainforest undergrowth at 200–700 m; rare. Map: Fig. 11.
- **GBIF:** 8 records (all specimens, 3 georeferenced), 1933–1961; elevation 240–1,250 m, median 457 m.
- **iNaturalist:** 2 observations (one wild, 2012; one cultivated, 2025), one research grade; 4 CC BY-NC photos, all copied.

## Notes

- "*Cyrtosperma carrii* would seem to have horticultural potential" (Hay 1988: 450): a small plant with a broad, richly coloured, net-veined spathe. The 2025 cultivated iNat record suggests it is now grown.
