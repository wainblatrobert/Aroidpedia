SPECIES DESCRIPTION:
⚠ To be taken from the protologue. The diagnostic character given in the title is downward-pointing spines, unique in the genus: in Hay's 1988 key spines mixed straight and down-turned characterise Podolasia, while Cyrtosperma has spines straight and up-turned or all up-turned, so this species breaks that distinction.² ⁴

INFLORESCENCE:
⚠ To be taken from the protologue. Placed by Hariri et al. (2024) in the Macrotum group, whose spathe is not convolute below and whose flowers are tetra-, penta- or hexamerous.⁵

ECOLOGY:
⚠ To be taken from the protologue; lowland forest of the Sorong region.²

ETYMOLOGY:
For Alistair Hay, monographer of the genus and author of six of its species.

NOTES:
1. The fourth member of the Macrotum group, with C. macrotum, C. beccarianum and C. prasinispathum, and like the last described from the Sorong region of Southwest Papua in 2024.⁵
2. Cyrtosperma prasinispathum also carries some downward-pointing spines among mostly upward ones,⁵ so the character is a matter of degree between the two Sorong species.
3. A single wild iNaturalist observation from 2026 is the only photograph available to the build.³
